Spectres contains a list of spectres that are powerful and their respective info (buff's, debuff's to enemies and auras). Also contains direct links to Wiki page (raise spectre skill page, each spectre page and the they provide auras).
Spectres
  • Perfect Judgemental Spirit: Cast Discipline and grant additional fire and chaos damage to attacks to all constructs (such as skeletons and raging spirits).
  • Perfect Hydra: Cast Hatred and can be used as a dps spectre.
  • Perfect Forest Warrior: Grants permanent Onslaught to all nearby allies in a wide radius, and has Culling Strike.
  • Perfect Guardian Turtle: Cast Determination and grant Rongokurai's Boon, wich occasionally applies a damage immunity buff to owner.
  • Perfect Primal Thunderbird: Cast Grace and applies -20% Resistance Lightning Exposure to enemies through Screech of Splendor.
  • Perfect Spirit of Fortune: Cast Wrath and Lightning Damage of nearby allies is Lucky.
  • They of Tul: Cast a totem wich applies Snow Cloak (15% chance to Avoid All Damage from Hits) to allies within the vortex.
  • Pale Seraphim: Applies 15% increased Damage taken and 15% reduced action speed to enemies.
